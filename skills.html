Design approach

From problem to polished interface

  1. Step 1

    Understand

    Business goals, user needs and constraints.

  2. Step 2

    Define

    Name the problem and prioritise requirements.

  3. Step 3

    Explore

    Organise content, map user flows, sketch wireframes.

  4. Step 4

    Design

    Visual system, responsive screens and prototypes.

  5. Step 5

    Refine

    Review usability, accessibility and feedback.

How deep each step goes depends on the project's scope and timeline.
