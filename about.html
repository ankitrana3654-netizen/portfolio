About me

Hi, I'm Ankit. I design interfaces people enjoy.

I'm a UI/UX designer focused on website interfaces, responsive experiences and accessible digital products. I currently work as an Associate UI Designer at Compunnel Technology.

My story

A path into design built on curiosity

I studied commerce at Delhi University and began my career in banking operations at Genpact, working on the Westpac Bank process.

Along the way I became curious about how digital products are designed, and why some interfaces feel effortless while others get in the way. That curiosity led me to train in UI/UX design and earn the IBM Enterprise Design Thinking Practitioner certification.

Today I work as an Associate UI Designer at Compunnel Technology on website redesigns and digital design projects. I enjoy the whole journey, from organising content and sketching wireframes to building polished, responsive screens in Figma.
